# Campus Sentinel live activity repair — 12 October 2026

The repaired native app is running at http://localhost:3000 with a clean camera overlay and `ACTIVITY_DEBUG=false`. General Entrance is online. Classroom is reconnecting: its currently saved source, `http://192.168.1.10:8080/video`, returns a connection timeout. Its record, source, and configuration were not changed in this repair. The older performance baseline used a different Classroom source and cannot establish a controlled comparison for that camera.

General Entrance confirmed **SLEEPING and TALKING in live samples**. PHONE USE and EATING did **not** confirm during the captured tests. No detections were injected into live state or PostgreSQL to make these tests pass. The available phone test contained no detected cell phone; eating inputs were limited by missing/off-frame wrists.

## Activity execution and results

All rows reuse the existing YOLO `track_id`. General Entrance's MediaPipe person analysis is enabled by `ENABLE_ACTIVITY_ANALYSIS_ON_GENERAL=true`; Classroom uses the same person analysis. `zone_type == "classroom"` alone enables classroom attendance, engagement, CAI, missing-student and attention metrics. General Entrance never calls `ClassroomMonitor` or persists classroom metrics. Activity and behavior intervals are nominally 0.75 seconds, limited by processing throughput; Classroom additionally samples its metrics on every 30th captured frame. Models are reused rather than loaded per frame.

## Requested findings

1. **False RUNNING:** Unsustained bounding-box motion and timing/coordinate inconsistencies could turn detector jitter into speed evidence. The replacement rule uses actual timestamps, reference-width scaling, EMA position smoothing, median speed, minimum history, sustained samples and displacement. Large geometry jumps clear history.
2. **False SUDDEN ACCELERATION:** A large ratio against a near-zero baseline is insufficient evidence. The rule now also requires a meaningful baseline, absolute speed increase, history and consecutive samples.
3. **Open-eye calibration:** 21 usable samples; EAR **0.235146–0.364670**, mean **0.308463**. The subsequent eyes-open control observed **0.244486–0.382418**.
4. **Closed-eye calibration:** 34 usable samples; EAR **0.230173–0.284762**, mean **0.264647**.
5. **Final sleeping threshold:** General Entrance **0.284903**, selected from the labeled calibration by maximizing observed true-candidate rate minus false-candidate rate. The original sample set yielded 100% closed-eye candidates and 4.76% open-eye candidates. Distributions overlap: this is a small camera-specific calibration, not proof of universal reliability. The 15-second temporal requirement remains. Classroom stays at its uncalibrated default 0.20.
6. **Sleeping live confirmation:** Yes. The first capture contained 27 confirmed samples. A later synchronized capture created real low-severity alert `abd38462-c7b7-41ff-9b7f-11ad691c5344`. The eyes-open control confirmed neither sleeping nor talking.
7. **Talking regression:** Threshold **0.035 unchanged**. The successful live capture had MAR stddev **0.011790–0.068746**, with 22/28 confirmed samples. Eyes-open/quiet control MAR stddev was **0–0.008329**. A later repeat had 0/29 confirmations; positive behavior cannot be promised for every pose or captured interval. Stored talking alert: `5792b521-53b0-4cbd-92f4-8bdbf9f7875d`.
8. **Raw phone evidence:** The supplied earlier live-frame baseline showed COCO **class 67**, confidence **0.408076**; another earlier observation was 0.604690. These are baseline measurements, not detections from the final phone capture. The final capture found no phone and therefore reported no phone confidence.
9. **Phone threshold:** **0.25** at inference and phone postfiltering, independent of the retained **0.50** person threshold. Full-frame target classes are explicitly restored after phone-only ROI calls, preventing Ultralytics' retained prediction options from accidentally removing persons.
10. **ROI fallback:** Available with the same locked YOLO model, bounded to two eligible person crops and at most 1280px enlargement. Crops preserve aspect ratio and coordinates map back to the original frame. It requires usable association landmarks; the final phone capture lacked wrists, so it could not demonstrate an eligible ROI rescue. A desk phone or ambiguous assignment remains unconfirmed.
11. **PHONE USE live-confirmed:** **No.** `no_cell_phone_detected` in all 27 final phone samples. Timing was not reduced to hide the missing detector input.
12. **Eating wrist visibility:** The representative final sample had left wrist **0.121948**, right wrist **0.226372**, both outside the crop; shoulders **0.998642 / 0.998491**. Pose acceptance remains **0.60**. Padding cannot recover arms outside the image. A cached MediaPipe Hands fallback supplies actual visible wrists when pose wrists are absent; ambiguous/outside-person points are rejected.
13. **Eating state transitions:** Explicit `AWAY → APPROACHING → NEAR_MOUTH → LEAVING`, counted only after a valid return away. Each hand has state; either usable hand suffices. Short landmark loss retains completed history, but interrupted transitions cannot create phantom cycles. Live reasons included `missing_mouth_wrist_or_shoulder_landmarks`, `insufficient_completed_cycles`, and `sample_gap_interrupted_cycle`.
14. **EATING live-confirmed:** **No.** Maximum 0 complete cycles in the final 20-sample capture. The debug-only one-cycle flag never substitutes for the production three-cycle requirement.
15. **Loitering coordinates:** Actual frame dimensions now normalize pixel positions before comparison with the fractional radius. Movement beyond the allowed radius starts a new duration episode.
16. **Restricted-area configuration:** Both existing cameras have **0 active restricted polygons**. The display name is RESTRICTED AREA; the underlying zone rule is retained. Legacy 640×360 zone-drawer coordinates normalize before checking the foot point. No camera, polygon or DB schema was recreated.
17. **Crowd detector:** Tests verified stale exclusion and growth from zero. No real density/surge positive test was performed. Camera events appear only in top banners.
18. **QR persistence:** Identity now lasts for the visible lifetime of its existing track, rather than expiring merely because the QR disappears for 30 seconds. Missing-track grace is 3 seconds; longer disappearance, restart/session reset clears identity. A roll number owned by another live/grace-protected track cannot be transferred by an ambiguous observation.
